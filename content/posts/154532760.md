---#### 1.找到在flutter下面的\\flutter\\packages\\flutter\_tools\\lib\\src\\web

![](https://i-blog.csdnimg.cn/direct/c55e6281c1f042deb778be0c1bdf497d.png)

![](https://i-blog.csdnimg.cn/direct/4c0640a780c14aa987815d4ac2d52b63.png)

在下图位置添加：

> '--disable-web-security',

![](https://i-blog.csdnimg.cn/direct/f3bc08bffc264cfca761dc06609d7854.png)

#### 2.删除flutter/bin/cache/下flutter\_tools.snapshot和flutter\_tools.stamp

![](https://i-blog.csdnimg.cn/direct/cfe558c7c5db4301ac55ad2d442ee51f.png)

#### 3.重新运行项目

执行：

> flutter doctor -v
